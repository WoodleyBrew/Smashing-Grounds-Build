Stage Select Screen:

	* Page layouts have been adjusted to include all stages on build, regardless of which stage preset used all stages will be available to play

	* All 1:1 alts from Vanilla and prior SG Builds have been moved to either L,R,Z or R+Start alts on a stage's slot. For example: Spear Pillar's R alt, a BF 1:1, has been removed from Spear Pillar and added to Battlefield as a R+Start alt. Several 1:1 alts for SV, GHZ, and DL have been removed as they did not function with the hazards toggle (and therefore weren't legal 1:1's)

	* All legal stages contain L, R, and Z alts that are 1:1's of the regular stage. In addition these alts are available in the R+Start list, able to be used with the Random Alt toggle option in the debug menu!

	* Many stages from Vanilla that were missing have returned, as either L+Start alts or standard button alts on stages.

		- L+Start alts always contain alternative stages, usually for fun / non-competitive stages

		- R+Start alts always contain 1:1 stages, Ex: All Battlefield's R+Start alts are 1:1 skins of Battlefield

	* Stock icons now appear on the stage selection screen, these tell you what character a player is playing so you won't have to back out to the CSS to check anymore
